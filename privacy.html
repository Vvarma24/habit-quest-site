Habit Quest – Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Habit Quest is a habit tracker that turns daily habits into quests. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iOS or Android.

The short version

Habit Quest does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in its own private storage on your device:

  • The habits (quests) you create and when you complete them
  • Your progress: XP, gold, streaks, badges, rewards, and companion
  • Hydration entries and reminder settings
  • App preferences

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Backups, export, and import

If you use Export backup in Settings, a file containing your Habit Quest data is created and saved or shared only where you choose. Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud Backup or Android backup) may include app data according to your device settings; those backups are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all Habit Quest data from your device.

Children

Habit Quest does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
