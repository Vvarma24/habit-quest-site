Habit Quest – Support

Need help with Habit Quest, or want to report a bug or suggest a feature? Email vjvarmaai@gmail.com. Please include your device model and operating system version.

Common questions

How do I add a habit?

On the Quest Log, tap the add button, give the quest a name, pick its details, and tap Add Quest.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed for Habit Quest in your device's Settings, and that reminders are switched on inside the app.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Habit Quest has no account and no cloud sync, so your data is not sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Use Settings → Export backup first if you want to keep a copy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, use Settings → Export backup. Transfer the file, then use Settings → Restore from backup on the new phone.
